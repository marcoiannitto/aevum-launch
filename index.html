The story

Almost two decades. Never advertised.

A close friend, PhD in Biochemistry with a unique passion for anti-ageing, spent almost two decades developing and refining a protocol. Over the years, thousands of people discovered it almost entirely by referral.

He asked if I wanted to try it. I did. It was unlike anything I'd come across.

Later, I asked him why he'd never made it public. He's a scientist, not a business person.

So I asked myself: why should something this good stay private?

Two years later, we built AEVUM.
